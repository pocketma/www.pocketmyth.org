Rock types and the Elite Four route

Rock is the cheapest mono-type team to build and the worst into grass. Pick it knowing that.

Why rock

Rock units are common, cheap to star up, and hit hard enough to end fights early. The whole team can be assembled from content you already clear.

The weakness

Grass and water both punish a rock lineup. Keep one non-rock coverage slot, even in the cup, or accept the bad matchup.
