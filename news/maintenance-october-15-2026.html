October 15 maintenance targets server load

This window is about stability rather than content. Expect smoother queues and fewer disconnects afterwards.

Why it matters

Server-load patches rarely add anything visible, but they change how ranked queues behave at peak hours. If you have been losing rating to disconnects, this is the patch that should help.

What to check afterwards

Play one ranked set at your usual hour. If disconnects persist, the issue is local rather than server-side.
