October 29 maintenance opens Season 12

Season 12 lands with the October 29 patch. Ranked resets, and last season's rewards are mailed out.

What resets

Ranked ratings roll over with the patch. Last season's placement rewards are mailed rather than paid instantly, so a quiet inbox right after the window is normal.

What to do first

Re-run your placement matches before the ladder fills up. Early-season matchmaking is loose, and a fast start is worth more than a perfect team.

Season openings usually carry small balance edits. Read them before you rebuild a lineup around last season's numbers.
