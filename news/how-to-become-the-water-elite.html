How to take the Water Elite title

Water is the most contested type in the cup. Winning it means countering the counter.

What the field looks like

Most water teams are built to beat fire and ground. That makes grass and electric the two types you must answer before anything else.

The winning slot

One ice or flying coverage move on the carry is usually enough. It is a smaller investment than a dedicated counter and it surprises players building for the mirror.
