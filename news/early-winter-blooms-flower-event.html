Early winter flowers bring a fresh exchange shop

The flower events are the most reliable free currency in the game. Here is how to clear the shop without wasting a day.

What it is

A flower-themed event with a sign-in track, a tribute mechanic and an exchange shop. The shop is where the value sits; the sign-in track is just the entry ticket.

How to clear it

Collect tributes in batches rather than spending them as they arrive. The shop prices do not move, so holding currency until you can buy the top item in one pass beats buying cheap items early.
