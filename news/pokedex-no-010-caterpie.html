Pokedex 010: Caterpie is a sleep button

The weakest-looking entry in the dex is also the cheapest source of sleep in the early game.

Role

Butterfree's sleep powder is the single most useful early utility move. It turns unwinnable gym fights into routine ones.

Investment

Do not star it up. Raise it to the level that unlocks sleep powder and leave it there.
