A free-to-play roster that still clears content

Which Pokémon are worth the investment, where every resource comes from, and two dungeon plans that work without spending.

Shiny first

Outside of UR, raise the shiny version of whatever you build. The flat 10 percent bonus compounds over a season and costs you nothing but a shiny perfume.

Cheap and effective

Butterfree, Gyarados, Dragonite, Gholdengo, Skeledirge, Venusaur, Clodsire and Garganacl all pull their weight. Gyarados works at every stage of the game, and Dragonite shifts from damage to support as your roster fills out.

Where resources come from

Held items come from dungeons and events; do not burn evolution stones on experiments. Friendship arrives through Trial Grounds and the guild shop. EVs come from the berry shop, ladder, honour shop and release shop. Bank about a thousand capsules before you start rerolling individual values, and buy tier-three and tier-four charms only on discount.

Two plans that work

Attrition: Mega Banette holding a Life Orb or a needle, Trick to steal the opponent's item, then Pyukumuku spamming Pain Split. Damage: Grapploct drops defence, a couple of Whimsicott drop attack, then your carry comes in to close.

Bank event currency

Card flips, flower events and wishing stars all repeat. Save the currency until you can clear a shop in one pass instead of trickling it out.
