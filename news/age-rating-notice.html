Age rating and player protection notice

The rating, the spending rules and the protections that apply to younger players.

The rating

POCKET MYTH carries an age rating suitable for teenagers and up. The rating is displayed on the store listing and inside the game menu.

Spending protection

Accounts under the age threshold are limited in how much they can spend per transaction and per month, and login windows are restricted for the youngest group under local rules.

Why this page exists

It is a compliance notice rather than a game update. If you are a parent, the account settings page is where limits are set.
