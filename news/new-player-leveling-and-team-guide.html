Levelling fast in POCKET MYTH without wasting a week

The starter decision, the monthly card question, a sandstorm team that builds itself, and the daily checklist.

Opening and spending

Starter choice is cosmetic; all three can be caught later. The monthly card is the best value on the store for a new account, and the shiny Greninja from the first recharge is strong enough to carry early content.

Who to raise

Dragonite is handed to everyone and stays useful as a pseudo-legendary. Gyarados arrives through events often enough that starring it up is cheap. There is also a shard trick: evolve an SSR juvenile at two stars and dismantle it for 25 shards, or take a UR to three stars before dismantling.

The sandstorm core

Clefable with Baton Pass, Gigalith to set sand, Excadrill to double its speed inside it, and Garganacl as the wall. Cheap to build, quick to finish, and stable in PvE.

Daily checklist

Spend your gacha tickets on generation three and the Groudon, Kyogre and Rayquaza banners. Farm six Pokémon Star a day, nine with the monthly card. Donate gold five times for the guild. Buy one gold pack for ten diamonds daily for the quest experience.

Dungeon picks

Banette is the practical Dynamax pick. Trial Grounds is rough for new accounts, so pair a support like Whimsicott with your carry rather than forcing a solo.
