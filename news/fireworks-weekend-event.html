A fireworks weekend with a short reward track

Weekend-only events are dense but unforgiving. Miss a day and you lose the top reward tier.

The catch

Weekend events usually gate the final reward behind attendance, not points. If you can only play one day, log in on the last day rather than the first.

What to prioritise

Claim the login reward, then the first two shop tiers. The third tier almost always costs more currency than it returns.
