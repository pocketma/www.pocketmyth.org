Before You Download

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything trainers usually ask before their first battle.

Is POCKET MYTH free to play?

Yes. POCKET MYTH is free to download and play on iOS and Android. Optional in-app purchases are available for cosmetic items and convenience packs.

Which platforms does POCKET MYTH support?

POCKET MYTH runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Use the Download button on this page to get the build for your device.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

Yes. POCKET MYTH is an online MMO, so a stable connection is required for battles, raids and seasonal events.

How many Pokémon generations are included?

Every generation is represented, from the Kanto starters to the latest regions, so you can build a team that spans the whole series.

What battle modes are available?

Turn-based single battles, tactical double battles, Mega Evolution, Terastal and Dynamax raids, plus seasonal ranked ladders.

Is POCKET MYTH available in my language?

The official site is available in English and Simplified Chinese, with more languages planned. In-game language support may vary by region.

Where can I find POCKET MYTH gift codes?

All current codes are listed on our gift codes page. Codes are limited by server and event window, so check that page before redeeming.

About the Game

A Monster-Collecting MMO Built for Mobile

POCKET MYTH is a 3D monster-collecting MMO for iOS and Android. Explore open routes, catch and train creatures from every generation, and take on gyms, seasonal events and other trainers in real time. Each partner has its own type, ability and evolution path, so no two teams play the same way.

Battles are turn-based and reward preparation over luck. Stack Mega Evolution, Terastal and Dynamax on top of classic type matchups, then test the result in tactical double battles and the seasonal ranked ladder. Between fights, upgrade badges and attributes, chase treasure events and build the team that fits your style.
